### The contract — the requesting session NEVER births the initiative

BIRTH mode creates **nothing**: no PocketBase row, no charter, no deliverable. It only spawns the
overseer, handing it a short prompt that **points at the brief file** (the body is never inlined) and
tells it to run `become --self` in its first turn.

This is deliberate, and it is the load-bearing half of the design:

- **The acts and the seat can never disagree.** Whoever performs the four acts must BE the overseer —
  `csm_initiatives.overseer_session_uuid` is what makes a session an overseer (`csm-overseer-definition-1`).
  A requesting session that wrote the row would name a seat it does not occupy.
- **A pre-brainstorm session is not the fleet.** It hands off and walks away; the overseer owns the
  initiative's whole life. Nothing is left half-created if the requesting session ends mid-turn.
- **The brief stays a file.** Pointer prompts survive the prompt-size ceiling that silently landed
  ZERO wards before `prompts-are-file-driven-1`.

### Machine detection — Mac delegates to DO, automatically

`detectMachine()` reads `LHI_MACHINE_ID` first (explicit wins), else the hostname. On a Mac the whole
spawn is delegated over `ssh lhi-digitalocean`, and any `/lhi_scripts/…` path (the brief, the cwd) is
translated to its DO-side equivalent. On DO it spawns locally. Unrecognised hosts are treated as Mac —
that fails safe (an extra ssh hop to the canonical runtime, never a fleet housed on the wrong machine).
